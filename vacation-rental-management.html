Vacation Rental Management

A future where SHINKA standards make every stay a confident choice.

Our Purpose & Vision

Why SHINKA is developing this service — Establishing quality standards through our own experience.

We believe in setting standards through firsthand experience, creating a future where travelers can confidently choose accommodations that meet SHINKA's quality standards.

Our approach to vacation rental management goes beyond simply handling daily operations. We begin by establishing exceptional quality standards at our own properties, paying close attention to every detail — from cleaning, amenities, and safety management to professional photography and multilingual guest support.

Once our methods and standards are thoroughly tested and established, we aim to share them with like-minded accommodation owners and real estate partners. Our vision is to build a trusted network where guests can enjoy a comfortable, worry-free stay at any property that meets SHINKA's standards.

Note: This business is currently in the planning stage, with future expansion in mind.

Our Key Initiatives

What we create, offer, and develop.

  • 01 / Quality Standard

    Setting High Standards at Our Properties

    We develop and implement comprehensive operating standards covering cleaning procedures, linen and amenity management, safety and hygiene, and multilingual guest support.

    These standards are carefully tested and refined at our own properties to ensure a consistently high-quality guest experience.

  • 02 / Value Creation

    Enhancing Property Branding & Guest Experience

    We develop effective ways to enhance each property's appeal through thoughtful interior design, professional photography and video, and compelling property listings.

    By highlighting the beauty and unique character of each space, we aim to create a distinctive identity and elevate the overall value of the accommodation experience.

  • 03 / Future Expansion

    Expanding SHINKA Standards to Partner Properties

    In the future, we plan to extend our proven management practices to partner properties and local accommodation providers.

    Our vision includes introducing SHINKA quality certification and comprehensive management support, building a trusted accommodation network that travelers can choose with confidence.
