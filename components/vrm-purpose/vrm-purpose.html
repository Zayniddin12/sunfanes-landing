Our Purpose & Vision

Why SHINKA is developing this service — Establishing quality standards through our own experience.

We believe in setting standards through firsthand experience, creating a future where travelers can confidently choose accommodations that meet SHINKA's quality standards.

Our approach to vacation rental management goes beyond simply handling daily operations. We begin by establishing exceptional quality standards at our own properties, paying close attention to every detail — from cleaning, amenities, and safety management to professional photography and multilingual guest support.

Once our methods and standards are thoroughly tested and established, we aim to share them with like-minded accommodation owners and real estate partners. Our vision is to build a trusted network where guests can enjoy a comfortable, worry-free stay at any property that meets SHINKA's standards.

Note: This business is currently in the planning stage, with future expansion in mind.
