Kokoro Karo's Role

  • 01

    Connecting People and Stories

    Through social media, Japanese culture articles, local reporting, the brand website, and accommodations, Kokoro Karo connects SHINKA's different activities and shares their stories across multiple channels.

  • 02

    A Friendly Travel Companion

    Rather than being a teacher with all the answers, she explores questions together with travelers and shares the excitement of new discoveries, just like a friend traveling alongside them.

  • 03

    Sharing the Joy of Discovering Japan

    Instead of focusing only on promoting SHINKA's services, she introduces lesser-known Japanese traditions, local destinations, and the unique experiences that make traveling in Japan so special.
