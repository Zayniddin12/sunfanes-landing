Our Key Initiatives

What we create, share, and develop.

  • 01 / Social Media & Web Articles

    Sharing Japanese Culture

    We introduce international travelers to Japan's culture, history, customs, cuisine, local communities, traditions, and everyday life through multilingual web articles and social media platforms such as Instagram and TikTok.

    Our goal is to make Japan's rich culture accessible, engaging, and easy to understand.

  • 02 / Fieldwork & Interviews

    Discovering Stories Firsthand

    Rather than relying solely on information available online, our editorial team visits destinations and speaks directly with local artisans and residents.

    Through these firsthand encounters, we create carefully researched stories that capture the authentic atmosphere, local heritage, and traditions of each place.

  • 03 / Virtual Human Collaboration

    Meet Our Virtual Human, “心カロ”

    Through our AI-powered virtual human, "心カロ", we provide helpful travel guidance and share engaging content on social media.

    By connecting digital experiences with real-world discoveries, we bring Japan's unique culture and experiences closer to travelers.
