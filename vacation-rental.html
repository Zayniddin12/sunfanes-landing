TOP / Vacation Rental & Accommodation

Vacation Rental & Accommodation

A private Japanese-modern retreat, where local heritage and deep relaxation come together.

Our Purpose & Background

Why SHINKA entered the accommodation business and how it connects to our brand philosophy.

Creating stays that make travel feel more effortless.

Away from the noise of the city, we create peaceful spaces where guests can unwind and clear their minds.

In today's information-filled world, what we truly need is not more stimulation, but a place where we can feel safe and at ease. SHINKA launched its vacation rental and accommodation business to offer just that — a comfortable retreat with room to breathe.

Rather than simply renovating buildings, we create accommodations that preserve traditional craftsmanship and architectural beauty for future generations.

By respecting the character of historic Japanese townhouses and traditional homes, and removing unnecessary decoration, we bring together refined simplicity, peaceful surroundings, and a deep sense of comfort.

Our Key Initiatives

What we create, develop, and offer.

  • 01 / Architecture

    Restoring Traditional Japanese Homes

    We carefully restore traditional Japanese townhouses and historic homes, improving their structural safety and insulation to modern standards while preserving the character of aged earthen walls and wooden beams.

    Each renovation respects the property's history and local heritage, creating spaces that carry traditional architectural beauty into the next generation.

  • 02 / Service

    Thoughtfully Designed Spaces & Services

    We combine convenient smart self-check-in with attentive support from a dedicated remote concierge, giving guests the privacy they value without sacrificing personal care.

    Our approach delivers warm, high-quality hospitality even without face-to-face interaction.

  • 03 / Experience

    Cultural Experiences with Local Artisans

    We collaborate with local tea ceremony practitioners, ceramic artisans, and traditional Japanese confectionery makers to offer exclusive private workshops for our guests.

    Through carefully selected furnishings and special amenities, visitors can discover and experience the unique culture of each destination.
