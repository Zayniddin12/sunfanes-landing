Virtual Human (VH) Introduction

Meet SHINKA's AI virtual human, working as a public relations representative. She connects SHINKA's social media, Japanese culture articles, on-site reporting, brand website, and accommodations.

Meet Kokoro Karo

A friendly companion who explores questions with travelers and shares the wonders of Japan.

Kokoro Karo isn't a teacher who claims to know everything about Japan. Instead, she's more like a friend who helps travelers find answers and discover what makes Japan so fascinating.

Rather than simply promoting SHINKA, she focuses on helping visitors discover Japan and enjoy their journeys, offering a friendly presence along the way.

Kokoro Karo's Role

  • 01

    Connecting People and Stories

    Through social media, Japanese culture articles, local reporting, the brand website, and accommodations, Kokoro Karo connects SHINKA's different activities and shares their stories across multiple channels.

  • 02

    A Friendly Travel Companion

    Rather than being a teacher with all the answers, she explores questions together with travelers and shares the excitement of new discoveries, just like a friend traveling alongside them.

  • 03

    Sharing the Joy of Discovering Japan

    Instead of focusing only on promoting SHINKA's services, she introduces lesser-known Japanese traditions, local destinations, and the unique experiences that make traveling in Japan so special.

The Story Behind Kokoro Karo

Sharing the hidden wonders of Japan and helping travelers find answers along the way.

Through welcoming international guests, SHINKA realized that many aspects of Japanese culture, local communities, people, and technologies remain relatively unknown. Travelers also often encounter things they don't know or understand.

To help bridge this gap, SHINKA began sharing Japanese cultural stories through online media, social platforms, and local reporting.
Kokoro Karo was created to bring these activities to life through friendly, personal communication.
